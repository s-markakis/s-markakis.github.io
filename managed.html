Questions
Is there a minimum contract?
No long lock-in. Plans are monthly — pause or cancel with 30 days' notice. Most hospitality clients stay year-round because the pre-season work is exactly what prevents the July breakdowns.
What happens if the internet drops?
On managed sites the monitoring alerts me the moment a line goes down — often before you notice. If failover is set up, the backup line takes over on its own and guests stay online. I investigate remotely first; if it needs hands on site, Hospitality clients get next-business-day response.
Remote or on-site support?
Most fixes are remote — config changes, reboots, updates, failover tuning. On-site is included on the Hospitality plan (next business day) and available on the other plans at €50/hour plus travel.
