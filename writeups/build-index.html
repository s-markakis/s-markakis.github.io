Build

Each post in this series takes one real kind of business and designs its network from nothing: the brief, the requirements, the VLAN and address plan, the policy matrix, every line of config, animated diagrams for anything that happens over time, failure drills before handover, and a bill of materials.

Build Series Real-World Design → Config Cisco MikroTik

The architecture guides explain patterns in the abstract, and the Cisco and MikroTik series teach one technology at a time. A Build post starts from a client brief instead, and every VLAN, ACL line and config block has to trace back to a requirement in it. That traceability is the point of the series. All of them are also filed under the build category on the blog.

Each one follows the same structure: brief → design decisions → address plan → policy matrix → build → verify & break → attacker's view → bill of materials. The core of each build also runs in Packet Tracer, and the post says which parts need CML or EVE-NG.
