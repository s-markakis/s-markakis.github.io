When Starlink is the right call

If your DSL sits at a few Mbps, drops every time the weather turns, or the provider keeps promising fibre "next year", Starlink is usually the honest fix — not another line from the same tired exchange. Speeds are comparable to a good city connection, and it works the same in a mountain village as it does on the coast.

For a business — a taverna taking card payments, rooms that advertise Wi-Fi, a farm running cameras — I pair it with your existing line so one goes down and the other carries on. Nobody at the till or in the rooms notices the switch.

What the installation includes

Site survey & obstruction check

Before anything is bought, I check the sky view from your actual roof — trees, ridgelines and neighbouring walls included — so you know it'll work before you spend a euro.

Mounting & weatherproofing

Roof, pole or wall mount that survives the meltemi and the winter storms. Cable runs sealed and tidy, not draped through a window.

Proper router integration

Your own router and Wi-Fi, sized for the building — not the basic dish setup. Guest network, VLANs and VPN if you need them.

Failover for businesses

Starlink plus your existing line, with automatic switchover so a dropped connection never stops a payment or empties the rooms of Wi-Fi.
