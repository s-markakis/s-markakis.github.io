Why it matters

On the booking platforms, Wi-Fi is one of the amenities guests filter for — and slow or dead Wi-Fi is one of the most common complaints in low-scoring reviews. A single "the internet didn't work" comment drags your average down and pushes you below the properties competing for the same guest.

The stone walls, split levels and outbuildings that make Cretan villas beautiful are exactly what a single router can't cover. Done right — with the access points placed for the building, not for the box's location — the connection is invisible: it just works, in every room, and nobody thinks to mention it.

What I set up

Whole-property coverage

Mesh or cabled access points sized to the building — thick walls, two floors, garden and pool included. No dead spots, no "move closer to the router".

Isolated guest network

Guests get their own network, separated from anything of yours — locks, cameras, your own devices. A simple password (or a printed QR card) and they're on in seconds.

Backup line for the busy months

For properties where an outage means angry guests, I add a second connection (4G or Starlink) that takes over automatically if the main line drops.

Monitoring & remote fixes

I watch the connection so you don't have to. If it goes down between guests, I usually know — and often fix it — before the next arrival walks in.
